# SOVEREIGN SAFE iOS User Guide

This guide provides instructions for using the **SOVEREIGN SAFE** wallet on your iOS device, ensuring you can manage your digital assets securely and efficiently.

---

## 1. Overview

**SOVEREIGN SAFE** is a non-custodial, multi-chain wallet that allows users full control over their digital assets without sacrificing simplicity or security.

---

## 2. Installation

1. Download **SOVEREIGN SAFE** from the App Store.
2. Complete the installation as you would with any standard iOS app.

---

## 3. Initial Setup

### Create a Wallet

- A 12–24 word recovery phrase will be generated.
- Write it down and store it securely offline.
- This phrase is the only way to recover your wallet.

### Secure Access

- Enable Face ID or Touch ID for secure access.
- Set up a fallback PIN for added security.

---

## 4. Interface Overview

### Dashboard
- Displays total portfolio value.
- Lists assets and their current balances.
- Shows recent activity for easy tracking.

### Navigation
- Assets are prioritized for user convenience.
- Chains are handled automatically by the app.
- **Settings** menu includes options for security, backups, and preferences.

---

## 5. Sending Assets

1. Select the asset you want to send.
2. Enter the recipient's address.
3. Choose the amount to send.
4. Review the fee and transaction speed.
5. Confirm the transaction with your biometrics.

---

## 6. Receiving Assets

- Select the asset you want to receive.
- Share your address or display the QR code to the sender.
- Funds will appear automatically after confirmation on the blockchain.

---

## 7. Managing Assets

- View balances for all supported chains and tokens.
- Add new tokens via the discovery feature in settings.
- Hide unused or low-balance assets from your dashboard.

---

## 8. Security & Recovery

- Private keys never leave your device.
- Use the recovery phrase to restore your wallet on any device.
- Loss of the recovery phrase means permanent loss of access to your assets.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting

- **Pending transactions**: May indicate network congestion.
- **Failed transactions**: Check for insufficient fees or balance.
- **App issues**: Restart the app or reinstall it using your recovery phrase.

---

## 10. Support

- Access in-app help for immediate assistance.
- Explore the knowledge base for common questions.
- Engage with community channels for additional support.

---

This user guide aims to empower you to utilize **SOVEREIGN SAFE** effectively, ensuring a secure and user-friendly experience managing your digital assets.
