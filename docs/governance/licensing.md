# SOVEREIGN SAFE — Governance & Licensing Model

This document outlines the governance framework and licensing model for the **SOVEREIGN SAFE** project, ensuring transparency, community engagement, and protection of intellectual property.

---

## 1. Governance Structure

### 1.1 Decision-Making

- **Core Team**: A designated core team is responsible for high-level decisions regarding the project’s direction, prioritization, and resource allocation.
- **Community Contributions**: Community members can propose features, enhancements, and bug fixes through GitHub issues and pull requests.
- **Voting Mechanism**: Key decisions (e.g., major feature integrations, protocol changes) will be subject to community voting, where stakeholders can express their preferences.

### 1.2 Roles and Responsibilities

- **Core Contributors**: Individuals who commit to actively maintaining components of the project, reviewing pull requests, and providing support.
- **External Contributors**: Individuals or teams who contribute features or fixes as needed but are not committed to ongoing maintenance.
- **Community Members**: Users and supporters who provide feedback, testing, and suggestions but do not engage in code contributions.

---

## 2. Licensing Model

### 2.1 Open Source License

The **SOVEREIGN SAFE** project will be licensed under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT), which allows for:

- Commercial use
- Modification
- Distribution
- Private use

The license facilitates broad adoption and collaboration while ensuring that contributions remain open and accessible.

### 2.2 Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

- Contributors must agree to a Contributor License Agreement before their contributions are accepted. This ensures that:
  - Contributors maintain ownership of their contributions.
  - The project retains a perpetual license to use, modify, and distribute contributions.

---

## 3. Community Engagement

### 3.1 Communication Channels

- **Official Repository**: GitHub will be the primary platform for   code contributions, issue tracking, and feature discussions.
  
- **Discussion Forum**: A designated forum (e.g., Discord, Reddit) for community discussions, feedback, and feature requests.
  
- **Regular Updates**: The core team will provide regular updates through blog posts or newsletters to keep the community informed about project developments and upcoming features.

### 3.2 Events and Meetups

- **Online Meetups**: Schedule regular online meetups for community members to discuss progress, share insights, and collaborate on projects.
  
- **Hackathons**: Organize hackathons to encourage community contributions and innovation, providing a platform for developers to showcase their work.

---

## 4. Transparency and Accountability

### 4.1 Documented Processes

- All major decisions, discussions, and changes will be documented in the project repository to ensure transparency.

### 4.2 Performance Metrics

- Regularly evaluate project performance using KPIs (e.g., contribution rates, issue resolution times) to hold the team accountable and identify areas for improvement.

---

## 5. Conclusion

The governance and licensing model for **SOVEREIGN SAFE** aims to foster a collaborative environment that encourages innovation and participation while protecting intellectual property. By engaging with the community and maintaining transparent processes, the project can grow sustainably and adapt to evolving user needs.

This document will be reviewed and adjusted periodically to reflect changes in the project and community dynamics.
